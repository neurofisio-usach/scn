The CAJAL Advanced Neuroscience Training Programme 2016

9 de diciembre de 2015HomeNoticias
Volver al archivo

Dear Colleague,

We would like to bring to your attention The CAJAL Advanced Neuroscience Training Programme, an initiative launched by FENS and IBRO, in partnership with the Champalimaud Foundation and Bordeaux Neurocampus.

Hereby we kindly ask your support in disseminating information about these hands-on training courses taking place in 2016 among your professional networks and colleagues.

You can spread information about this excellent initiative by:

· Forwarding the two below images to your colleagues and students (click on the pictures to download);
· Printing them yourself;
· Requesting to receive printed copies of the CAJAL 2016 courses programme poster and flyer (please fill in this form by 16 December at the very latest to update your postal address and inform us how many copies you would need).

Thank you for your support.

Kind Regards,
The CAJAL Central Administration
